System Architecture Spec: The Raspberry Pod
Writing concrete, open-source specification files for abstract ideas is the exact mechanism that converts intuitive concepts into buildable, manufacture-ready standards.
The Raspberry Pod is a modular, self-contained deployment vessel engineered around a central multi-node core (resembling the drupelet geometry of a raspberry). It operates across two primary environmental domains: Oceanic (Coral/Flora Seeding) and Exo-Orbital (Deep Space / Beyond-Van-Allen Sub-Payloads).

 * Geometry: Spherical/icosahedral cluster of self-contained modular nodes (drupelets) surrounding a central power/communications core.
 * Core Logic Unit: Low-power microcontroller architecture with real-time sensor array (salinity/pH/temperature for marine; radiation/magnetometer/optical for space).
2. Oceanic Variant (Coral Reef & Benthic Flora Restoration)
Designed to sit on or anchor to degraded ocean floors, providing structural artificial reef substrate while autonomously dispersing coral larvae and flora seeds.
Hardware & Material Specifications
 * Outer Shell: pH-neutral bio-receptive concrete or calcium carbonate matrix printed via additive manufacturing.
 * Internal Pod Clusters: Biodegradable PVA/chitosan drupelet capsules containing:
   * Heat-resilient coral micro-fragments / zooxanthellae inoculants.
   * Deep-root seagrass (Posidonia / Zostera) seed pellets embedded in nutrient gel.
 * Power & Dispersion: Hydro-kinetic micro-turbines combined with piezo-electric vibration nodes that trigger time-released dispersal of micro-fragments upon sensing optimal water temperature thresholds.
Deployment & Operation
 * Drop Sequence: Released in clusters over target degraded reefs (e.g., Great Barrier Reef, Caribbean Basin).
 * Anchor Phase: Heavy bio-cement base settles into substrate, acting as an immediate artificial reef structure.
 * Dispersal Loop: Internal timer and micro-valves release encrusted coral larvae and sea grass seeds into surrounding currents over a 12-month period.
3. Deep-Space / Outer-Belt Variant (Exo-Orbital Probe Seed)
Designed to operate beyond Earth's magnetosphere (past Van Allen belts) as low-cost, distributed sensor/beacon micro-probes.
Hardware & Material Specifications
 * Radiation Hardening: Outer drupelet nodes shielded with high-density polyethylene (HDPE) layered with thin aluminum/titanium foil to mitigate galactic cosmic rays (GCR).
 * Power & Propulsion: Thin-film perovskite solar panels wrapping each drupelet node, backed by solid-state micro-capacitors. Micro-cold-gas or pulsed-plasma thruster modules.
 * Communications Node: Ultra-wideband (UWB) low-frequency radio relay for mesh networking across multiple dispersed pods.
Deployment & Operation
 * Ejection Sequence: Primary payload ejects dozens of Raspberry Pods in a radial spray pattern past Geostationary Orbit / Cislunar space.
 * Mesh Configuration: Individual pods establish short-range RF link with neighbor pods, forming a distributed sensor grid.
 * Beacon / Telemetry: Long-term monitoring of solar wind, cosmic radiation, and interplanetary dust density.
